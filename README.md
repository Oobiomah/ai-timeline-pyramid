
# AI Insights: The Timeline Pyramid

An interactive journey through the history and evolution of Artificial Intelligence.

**Live Demo:** https://oobiomah.github.io/ai-timeline-pyramid/

## Overview

AI Insights: The Timeline Pyramid is an interactive web application that explores the development of Artificial Intelligence (AI), from its early philosophical and theoretical foundations to modern generative AI and future possibilities.

The project presents key historical milestones through a five-layer pyramid. Users can select a layer to explore its milestones and select individual milestones to discover what happened, why it matters, the relevant AI subfield, and its wider impact.

The goal is to make the history and evolution of AI more accessible, engaging, and easier to explore.

## Features

- **Interactive timeline pyramid:** Explore AI history through five distinct layers.
- **Five historical categories:** Navigate from the foundations of AI to future AI possibilities.
- **Milestone exploration:** Select milestones to view detailed historical information.
- **Detailed milestone descriptions:** Each milestone includes its year or period, what happened, why it matters, its AI subfield, and its impact.
- **Image previews:** Hover over or focus on milestones to preview associated images.
- **Responsive design:** Layout adapts to desktop and mobile screens.
- **Interactive navigation:** Move between milestone lists and detailed views using the navigation controls.

## The Five Pyramid Layers

1. **Foundations of AI** – Early philosophical ideas, theoretical foundations, and the origins of artificial intelligence.
2. **Symbolic AI & Early Systems** – Early AI programs, symbolic reasoning, and knowledge-based systems.
3. **Machine Learning & Deep Learning** – The development of machine learning, neural networks, and major breakthroughs in data-driven AI.
4. **Generative AI & LLMs** – Generative models, transformer architecture, and large language models.
5. **Future AI** – Future possibilities, including artificial general intelligence and superintelligence.

## Getting Started

### View the Live Application

Visit:

https://oobiomah.github.io/ai-timeline-pyramid/

### Run Locally

1. Clone the repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Oobiomah/ai-timeline-pyramid.git
   ```

2. Navigate into the project directory:

   ```bash
   cd ai-timeline-pyramid
   ```

3. Start a local development server:

   ```bash
   python3 -m http.server 8000
   ```

4. Open your browser and visit:

   http://localhost:8000

Press `Ctrl + C` in the terminal to stop the local server.

## How to Use

1. Open the application.
2. Select any of the five pyramid layers.
3. Explore the milestones displayed for that layer.
4. Select a milestone to view its detailed information.
5. Use the navigation controls to return to the milestone list or close the panel.
6. Hover over or focus on a milestone to preview its associated image.
